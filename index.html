What it is

WeazyGym Studio is a private tool made by and for the WeazyGym channel. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and is controlled through the owner's own Telegram chat.

It is not a public service. Only the owner of the WeazyGym channel signs in to it.

What it does

  • Turns finished WeazyGym audio tracks into YouTube videos, with a thumbnail, title and description.
  • Uploads those videos to the WeazyGym YouTube channel.
  • Reads the channel's own video list and stats (views, watch time, subscribers) so the owner can see which videos work.
