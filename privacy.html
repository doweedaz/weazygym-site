Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what WeazyGym Studio ("Studio", "we") does with data from your Google account. Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the WeazyGym YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data Studio accesses

When you sign in with Google, Studio asks for these permissions and nothing else:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos and their thumbnails to your channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read your channel's video list, titles, lengths and publish times.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read your videos' views, watch time, average view duration and subscribers gained.

Studio cannot delete or edit your existing videos. It does not access your email, contacts or any other Google service.

How the data is used

  • To upload the videos you choose to your own channel.
  • To show you reports about how your own videos perform.

The data is not used for ads, profiling, or training AI models, and it is not sold.

Where the data is stored

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on the channel owner's own computer. It is never uploaded to any server we run and is never stored in any code repository.
  • Video files, upload records and stats reports are stored on the same computer.
  • Studio has no server of its own and no database outside that computer.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your Google user data to anyone. The only place it is sent is the channel owner's own private Telegram chat with Studio, where reports and previews are shown. That message is handled by Telegram under Telegram's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data is kept only as long as it is useful for uploads and reports.
  • You can remove Studio's access at any time on your Google Account's third-party access page (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). After that, Studio can no longer read or upload anything.
  • To ask for stored data to be deleted, email the contact below. It will be deleted within 30 days.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
